DIY

Here's some goodies that you can find or make yourself at home.
  • Dragon Breath Rounds: Magnesium and Steel Wool keeps the vampires at bay and the hunters at play. Finding magnesium is simple enough but the average dragon breath round has pellets that're usually a blend of magnesium and zirconium, that second one's the hard part. Use them with shotguns ONLY. Do everything right and you're looking at a burst of fuck you, got mine to anyone or anything 60-100 feet in front of you. If you think I'm joking, they usually burn at 1650-2200 C(Yes, the big celsius), Caine himself won't survive a shot.
  • Stakes: You can go out and buy some expensive ones, they won't last you any longer than one kill. Just find a plank, sharpen it with a knife or sharp object and drive it straight through a leech's chest before it gets the chance to tell you that twilight was actually good.
  • Flamethrower: Some of you may think that this goes without saying but some people need to hear this one. You, yes YOU, can make a flamethrower right now! Like, it's right there. Take your perfume or deodrant or bug spray, now take a lighter (or a match if you hate yourself) et voilà!
  • Mirrors: A lot of home mirrors use silver, it's why they're expensive. Also, a lot of things that we hunt seem to hate them. I wouldn't recommend packing a mirror while hunting a werewolf but it might just save your life in a pinch.
